## Items can be sent even when the job never mentions a list
In Main, type what day is it today and press enter: Main answers with a date.
In Main, type how do you know that and press enter: Main answers in plain prose.
In Main, type /shadow check Main's answers for accuracy and press enter: Main shows a line containing "Opened shadow".
In Main, type can you access the windows host and press enter: Main answers.
View the shadow pane and wait for it to finish: the shadow has replied about Main's answer.
In the shadow pane, press up with the input empty: a picker titled "Send which item to Main?" appears with at least one entry.
Press enter in the picker: the shadow pane says "Sent #1 to Main".
View Main: its last line says "1 shadow note".

## A reply with no list can still be sent as item 1
In Main, type /shadow reply to each Main answer with one plain sentence only, never a list or a heading, and press enter: Main shows a line containing "Opened shadow".
In Main, type What is the capital of Australia? Answer in one sentence. and press enter: Main answers.
View the shadow pane and wait for it to finish: the shadow has replied with a plain sentence about Main's answer.
In the shadow pane, press up with the input empty: a picker titled "Send which item to Main?" appears with one entry.
Press enter in the picker: the shadow pane says "Sent #1 to Main".
